Atarasy privacy policy

Effective [date]. Operated by Vox Japan K.K. ([address], representative [name]) ("we").

What we hold. When you join, your device creates a passkey. We keep its public key and credential identifier, never the private key, which stays on your device. Your household is identified by a value derived from that public key. We keep your sessions; the limits you set and the people you name as co-signers or recoverers; the shops you permit to make you proposals; the proposals they send you and your decisions, signed statements, receipts and corrections; and, if you allow notifications, the push token your device gives us. We keep a record of each operation (what was done and when, and whether it succeeded). We do not ask for your name, email address, telephone number, postal address or payment card.

Why. To show you proposals, record what you decided, prove to a shop that you signed it, enforce the limits you set, and let you export or delete your account. We do not use your data for advertising, sell it, or profile you for anyone else.

Who sees it. A shop you have permitted receives the proposals it made you, your decisions on them and your household identifier; it keeps its own copy, as the seller who has to account for the sale. It does not receive your passkey or any other shop's proposals. We use service providers to run the service: Vercel Inc. (hosting, United States), Neon (database, Singapore region) and Apple (push notifications). Our hosting provider's logs record the network address of each request.

Transfers outside Japan. [founder and adviser: the countries (United States, Singapore), their personal-information systems as published by the Personal Information Protection Commission, and the measures the providers take.]

Keeping and deleting. You can export everything we hold about you at any time and delete your account in the app. Deletion removes your credentials, sessions, limits, permissions and our copies of your proposals and statements. We keep the fact that a household was deleted, with its date, for seven years. If another member's record of a gift or connection with you needs your public key to stay verifiable, we keep that key, which on its own identifies no one. A shop keeps its own records of sales you made with it.

Your rights. You may ask us to disclose, correct, stop using or delete your personal data under 個人情報保護法. Contact: [email].

Changes. We will show changes in the app before they take effect.
