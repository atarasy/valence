Atarasy support

Atarasy is operated by Vox Japan K.K. Membership is by invitation only. For help with your account, or to ask about an invitation, write to us at support@vox.delivery.

Use one device

While Atarasy is in its pilot, use it on one device. Your records are encrypted with a key that stays on the device where you first signed in, so that we cannot read them. Another device signed in to the same account, or the app deleted and installed again, cannot open them. If that happens, the app offers to delete the account; you can then join again with a new invitation. Your decisions and statements already made stay with the shops that received them.
